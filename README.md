# 🖨️ Print PW Notes

**By Bhaskar Kumar** — bhaskarkumar787878@gmail.com

🌐 **Visit:** [https://bhaskarsn.github.io/printpwnotes/](https://bhaskarsn.github.io/printpwnotes/)

A free, browser-based tool to convert Physics Wallah (PW) PDF notes into
print-ready files — without uploading anything anywhere.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🔒 100% Private
Your PDF never leaves your device. All processing runs entirely in your
browser. No server, no uploads, no data collection.

### 🎨 Normal Mode — Black-Background Slides
For standard PW slides with a black background and colourful text/diagrams.
Prints sharply without wasting ink on dark backgrounds.

### 📖 Embed Mode — Warm Bookish Questions
For pages with a yellowish warm-toned question image (e.g. a photo taken
from a book and pasted into the slide). Preserves the warm tone so questions
print clearly.

### 🗑️ Remove Mode — Delete Unwanted Pages
Mark any page as Remove to drop it from the final output. Perfect for cover
sheets, blank pages, or anything you don't need printed.

### ⚙️ Parallel Worker Control
Choose how many workers process your PDF in parallel. The app auto-selects
a sensible default based on your device.

### 📊 Live Progress
A real-time progress bar shows the current stage, percentage, and elapsed
time while your PDF is being processed.

---

## 🚀 How to Use

🌐 **Visit:** [https://bhaskarsn.github.io/printpwnotes/](https://bhaskarsn.github.io/printpwnotes/)

1. Open the website and drop your PW PDF (or click to browse)
2. The PDF opens in the page editor — every page is shown as a thumbnail
3. Tag each page:
   - **Normal** — standard black-background PW slide
   - **Embed** — warm yellowish question image pasted in
   - **Remove** — drop this page from output
4. Choose your worker count
5. Click **Process PDF** and wait for the progress bar to finish
6. Click **Download PDF** to save your print-ready file
