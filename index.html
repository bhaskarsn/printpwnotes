Made for Physics Wallah-style notes. After picking a file you can mark each page — leave it Normal for black-bg slides and image embedded with plain white background like pdf, pick Embed for pages with a yellowish question image like taken photo from book looks warm yellow is pasted in, or Remove to drop the page from the output.

Why Print PW Notes?

🔒
100% Private
Your PDF never leaves your device. Everything runs entirely in your browser — no uploads, no server, no data collection of any kind.
🎨
Zero Visibility Loss for Colourful Notes
Colourful PW slides are processed to keep all colours intact when printed. Black-background slides print cleanly without washing out your notes.
📖
Warm Bookish Questions? Use Embed
For pages with a yellowish warm-toned question image — like a photo taken from a book and pasted into slides — select the Embed option for that page to preserve the warm tone perfectly.
🗑️
Delete Pages Before Processing
Mark any page as Remove to drop it from the final output. Clean up unwanted pages, cover sheets, or blanks before downloading.
